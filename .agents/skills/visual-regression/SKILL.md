description: Verify bmatrix image clarity, palette modes, frame stability, and animation quality.

Read AGENTS.md. Use generated redistributable fixtures in CI and ignored personal images locally. Compare logical frames under fixed dimensions/time/seed rather than relying only on screenshots. Inspect green, monochrome, and full-color output, aspect ratio, highlights, dark features, whitespace, and temporal stability. Record what was visually inspected and the limits of preview fidelity. A synthetic browser or raster preview does not prove terminal-emulator behavior. Block release if visual or motion review is missing.
